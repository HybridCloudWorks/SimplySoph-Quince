# Pages and how the family uses them

All routes below exist. Guest routes have matching `/es/` versions and language links. Admin routes are family-only after the administrator sign-in (emailed link, Google or Microsoft, then an authenticator code); their static shells contain no private data. Who can sign in is decided in Notion: see [account-access.md](account-access.md).

## How guests find the pages

The header has one link per section, named the same as the page it opens: **Schedule**, **Travel** (once it has content, see below), **Photos**, **A note for Sophia**, **Gifts**, **Questions** and **Contact**, then **Sign in** (it opens My account and reads **My account** once signed in; administrators sign in at `/admin/login/` instead, which no guest page links to), the language switch (**Español** / **English**, shortened to **ES** / **EN** on phones) and **RSVP**. On screens narrower than 1080px the links sit behind a **Menu** button so the header stays one 64px row. A section's other pages (the ceremony and reception under Schedule; sharing under Photos) are listed under its page titles. The policies and message programs are in the footer.

**Decision (round 2, R-027): the note for Sophia has its own header item.** The review asked that "A note for Sophia" be reachable in at most two taps from any page on a phone, and that each header label match the page it opens. Round 2's first answer renamed the Photos section "Photos & notes" / "Fotos y mensajes" and kept the note as that section's third page, without a slot of its own (to keep the header as it was). That left the note three taps away on a phone (Menu, "Photos & notes", then the note) and gave the header an item that opened a page titled "Photos". So the note now has its own item, **A note for Sophia** / **Un mensaje para Sophia**, right after **Photos** / **Fotos**, whose label is again its page's title. It is the smallest change that meets the rule in every phase: the header is the same before, on and after the day, so the note is two taps away from every page (Menu, then the note) and one click away on a computer, and one tap from the pages that link it (the home page and both RSVP confirmations). It stays after January 15, unlike the review's pre-event menu (R-113, still not built), because notes are welcome after the party too (the home page then offers "Leave Sophia a note"). The wide header still fits one row at its narrowest, 1081px, in both languages; with Travel added it already took two rows in Spanish at that width before this change, and still does below about 1280px. `tests/browser/navigation.spec.mjs` counts the taps on a 390px phone, in English and Spanish.

Pages that wait for the family's words are built but not linked from anywhere until their content exists in `family` in `site/content.mjs` (`waitsFor` there decides): **Sophia** (her story, the parents' letter or the moments), the **court of honor**, the **padrinos**, **Travel** (hotels or the airport note) and, after the event, **Thank you** (the note or the highlight video). Once one is filled in, the next build adds a **Sophia**, **Travel** (in its place after Schedule, and linked from the Schedule's "Good to know") or **Thank you** link to the header and lists Sophia's filled pages together; nothing else needs changing. Visited directly while empty, these pages end with the RSVP and the schedule rather than a dead end (the RSVP button goes once the household has replied, and for everyone after the party, with the header's RSVP). `npm run check` fails if any page links to one that is still empty.

The court of honor and the padrinos are different: their content is people's names, and this repository is public. `family.court` and `family.padrinos` stay empty (`npm run check` fails if either gets an entry); names may only come from the runtime event settings (`server/site-settings.mjs`, `GET /api/site`), never from a commit. Building those two pages from the settings is not done yet, so they keep their waiting text ([publishing.md](publishing.md#names-stay-out-of-this-repository)).

| `/ceremony/` | Ceremony time and confirmed church/parking details when supplied; calendar files in the page's language (`/calendar/es/…` on Spanish pages) |
| `/reception/` | AMZ address and map directions |
| `/rsvp/` | Opens with the private invitation link, or the link or code pasted into the form (RSVP only), or for a signed-in guest once their household's link is issued. States the household's allocation from Notion and the reply-by date; **Everyone's coming** / **We can't make it** go straight to the review, as do the invitation email's quick-answer links; or per-event counts, asking only about the invited events (each with its time and place) and about children only when the invitation includes them. Nothing is saved before **Save my RSVP**. Answers in progress stay in the browser tab through a reload, a language switch or Back (kept per household, used only while they fit the invitation, and cleared by signing out), and while the guest keeps working the link session stays open (up to 2 hours) |

| `/gallery/` | **Photos**: the family's portraits, once supplied, open the page by album for everyone; then approved photos and videos, for signed-in guests. | `/faq/` | **Questions**: invitation allocation, the deadline with a link to the RSVP (after it, that RSVPs are closed), editing, photos and pending attire |
| `/guestbook/` | Well-wishes from signed-in guests; only approved messages are public |

Sign-in links, RSVP receipts and the registered SMS welcome after a first SOPHIA are the only messages to guests sent without a person pressing Send (owner decisions 7 and 16 in [v1/access-model.md](v1/access-model.md)); WhatsApp answers only the STOP, START and HELP keywords on its own, and Twilio answers SMS STOP and HELP. The family's own notification emails (to `NOTIFICATION_EMAILS`) are also automatic and never go to guests. They go only to an address on the household's Notion row, never to one typed into a form. Each household gets at most 5 automatic receipts a day; later ones wait as drafts in Communications. No bulk campaign or scheduled reminder is ever sent automatically. No meal choices are invented; menu selections require family-supplied options before implementation. Separate dietary/accessibility/song requests are currently collected in one optional request field.

A route being present does not imply its family content or provider account setup is complete. See [validation.md](history/validation.md).
